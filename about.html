Our Introduction

Welcome to Kevin’s Panciteria

We’re a restaurant dedicated to the many noodle dishes that Filipinos call pancit — stir-fried, soupy, saucy, or eaten straight off a banana leaf. You can find us at P.F. Alviar, Vintar, Ilocos Norte.

Filipino Pancit Traditions

Noodles That Bring Families Together

Pancit is woven into Filipino life — served at birthdays for long life, at fiestas for abundance, and at home for simple comfort. Chinese immigrants introduced noodle dishes to the Philippines generations ago, and Filipino communities have since adapted them with local ingredients, flavors, and customs found nowhere else.

Pancit Palabok & Pancit Malabon

Rich, saucy noodle dishes from Manila and Malabon, often prepared for special gatherings.

Pancit Molo

A dumpling soup from Iloilo’s Molo district, showing the influence of Chinese-Filipino cuisine.

Batil Patung & Habhab

Distinctive regional specialties from Cagayan and Quezon, each with its own serving tradition.
