Pancit Heritage

Every Region Has a Pancit Story.

Across the Philippines, pancit is more than a noodle dish — it's part of birthdays, fiestas, rainy afternoons, and everyday family meals. Each region has shaped pancit with its own local ingredients and traditions.

Luzon

Home to festive favorites like Pancit Palabok and Pancit Malabon, often served at fiestas and family celebrations across the island — including right here in Ilocos Norte.

Visayas

Iloilo's Pancit Molo reflects the region's long-standing Chinese-Filipino culinary influence, turning noodles into delicate dumpling soup.

Beyond the Islands

From Cagayan's Batil Patung to Quezon's hand-eaten Habhab, every community has adapted pancit to its own local flavors and customs.
